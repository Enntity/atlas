# Contribution licensing in Enntity/atlas

Enntity/atlas does not require contributors to sign a Contributor License
Agreement. Contributions to this fork are accepted under
[AGPL-3.0-only](LICENSE), as described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
No separate commercial relicensing grant is requested.

The earlier CLA text was inherited from upstream. It is retained in Git history
as a historical record, along with existing signature records; this policy does
not modify any agreement previously made by a contributor. An upstream
contribution is a separate action subject to the upstream repository's policy.
