## Merge verdict

**OK with notes** — this is analysis, not a diff; no code was changed and no correctness defect was found in any reviewed kernel. All findings above are performance/structural (P1/P2), and the highest-value items are configurations or ports of already-present sibling kernels rather than new numerics.

Two caveats the parent should carry forward:

- **The `kda_fla_*` ranking is conditional on turning that lane on.** In the measured stack it contributes nothing (F1). If the plan intends to enable `ATLAS_KDA_FLA_REFERENCE=1` for prefill parity, then items #10–#14 in the ranked table become live and #10 (the dead memsets) is a free deletion.
- **No kernel in the reviewed families is currently a top-3 target.** With mHC at 1.0 %, MLA/indexer at ~0.5 %, and KDA/SSM at ~0.14 % of the 84.9 s trace, a 10× matmul win would leave roughly 8.5 s of prefill in which mHC is ~10 %. That is the right time to land finding #1/#2 (M-tiling the mHC projection) — and it is the same fix as the brief's P1/P2 applied to the last remaining projection in the model.
